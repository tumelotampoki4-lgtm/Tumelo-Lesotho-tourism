Distination Tour Packages

Katse_Dam

The Katse Dam is a massive concrete wall built across the Malibamatšo River deep in the mountains of Lesotho. It stands 185 meters high, making it one of the tallest dams in Africa. It was built in the 1990s as part of a massive engineering plan called the Lesotho Highlands Water Project.

Thaba Bosiu Cultural Village

The sacred resting place for the kings and royal family of Lesotho. It holds the graves of the founding father King Moshoeshoe I, King Moshoeshoe II, and other prominent historical leaders.

Kome Caves

The Kome Caves are a historic village of beehive-shaped homes built directly inside a deep, natural sandstone rock overhang in the Berea District of Lesotho.
